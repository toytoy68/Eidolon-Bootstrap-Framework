How CAPTCHA challenges work

A CAPTCHA is a test meant to tell humans and automated programs apart. Sites may show a page saying "Checking your browser" or "Please enable JavaScript and cookies to continue" before letting a visitor in.

This article explains the history of these tests, their accessibility problems and why changing a User-Agent string does not reliably avoid them. It is an ordinary article, not a challenge page.

Accessibility: audio alternatives exist but are often criticised. Privacy: some challenges collect browser signals. Research: several studies measure how long people take to solve them.

Conclusion: a challenge page is an access decision by the site, and respecting it matters.
